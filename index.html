Meet Cube privacy policy

Publisher: S.Nagiba
Effective date: 19 October 2026

Meet Cube adds a cube, a 3D model or an image to your Google Meet camera video and lets you control it with your hands.

What the extension processes

The extension processes camera frames and temporary hand landmarks to place and manipulate objects. If you choose a local GLB or image, it reads that file and its filename to render it and label the object menu. Camera track settings are used to preserve capture behavior. Audio tracks pass through unchanged; Meet Cube does not analyze audio, transcribe conversations or identify people from their hands or faces.

Processing stays on your device

Hand detection, 3D rendering and image processing run locally using software bundled with the extension. S.Nagiba receives no camera frames, hand landmarks, imported files or meeting content from the extension. Meet Cube has no analytics, advertising trackers, account service or payment service.

Your meeting video

The object becomes part of the camera video sent to Google Meet. Google Meet transmits that video and your normal audio to meeting participants under its own permissions and policies. Meeting participants may see and, where their meeting tools allow it, record the composited content. This policy covers Meet Cube, not Google's services or other participants' actions.

Storage and retention

Frames, landmarks and imported object data are held temporarily in memory for the feature. Meet Cube does not record camera video or save these data to persistent storage. Imports are cleared when the Meet page is closed or reloaded. Selecting a file does not modify the original file on your computer.

Your choices

You choose which files to import. Freeze pauses object movement; Hide removes the object from the composed video. These are object controls, not camera or recording controls. Use Meet's camera control to stop sending camera video. To stop the extension from operating, disable or remove it in Chrome and reload the Meet page.

Use and sharing

Data are used only to provide the object and hand-control feature. The extension does not sell data or use it for advertising, unrelated profiling, lending or creditworthiness. Meet Cube's use of information received from Google APIs, if any, will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact and updates

Contact S.Nagiba at s.nagiba@proton.me for privacy or support questions. If this policy changes, an updated effective date will appear here. If you email support, the information you choose to include will be used to respond to your request.
